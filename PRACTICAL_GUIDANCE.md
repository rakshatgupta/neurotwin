# Practical next steps

The previous view mixed brief flag-based suggestions with a long list of testing caveats under one disclosure. This revision presents each data-dependent suggestion as a visible card: title, dated measurement and personal reference, then a specific next action. General testing cautions and professional follow-up remain available under See more.

Sleep, stress, movement, cognitive and recording changes are selected only from recent flagged comparisons. Steps/minutes changes share one movement card while preserving both reasons. A recent zero-hour sleep entry instead prompts confirmation before a sleep-routine inference. Forming, steady and missing/stale histories get explicit, non-diagnostic states rather than invented abnormalities or arbitrary targets. Recommendations do not require Groq or another paid API. Recorded baselines are descriptive, not personal health targets.

General habit sources: CDC sleep guidance (https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/); NHS breathing for stress (https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/); NHS walking (https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/walking-for-health/). Linked from applicable cards. These sources support general habits, not the clinical validity of NeuroTwin's flags or a claim that an action treats their cause.
